Türkiye Mining & Quarrying Accidents — a public record

This is a record of people who went to work in Türkiye's mines and quarries and did not come home. Every value traces to cited source documents through recorded reviewer decisions. It exists for remembrance and accountability — not comparison, not spectacle.

Records are seeded from open tertiary sources (Wikidata, Wikipedia) and await corroboration against official and professional-chamber sources. Coverage is partial: years without a mark are years without a published, reviewed record — not years without loss.

One mark is one person, in published incident records, oldest first. Rest on a group to see the incident.

Where it happened

Marker area reflects the number of people who died. Solid markers are facility-approximate locations — the mine or its vicinity, never an exact point. Hollow dashed circles summarize records without published coordinates at their province's reference point — a province-level summary, never an incident location. Select any marker to read what happened.
