Türkiye Mining & Quarrying Accidents — reviewed public records

An evidence-based record of fatal mining and quarrying accidents in Türkiye. Every value shown traces to cited source documents through recorded reviewer decisions. This page documents deaths; it is presented for remembrance and accountability, not comparison.

Current records are seeded from open tertiary sources (Wikidata, Wikipedia) and await corroboration against official and professional-chamber sources. Coverage is partial: years without a mark are years without a published, reviewed record — not years without loss.
