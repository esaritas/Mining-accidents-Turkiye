Türkiye Mining & Quarrying Accidents — a public record

This is a record of people who went to work in Türkiye's mines and quarries and did not come home. Every value traces to cited source documents through recorded reviewer decisions. It exists for remembrance and accountability — not comparison, not spectacle.

Records are seeded from open tertiary sources (Wikidata, Wikipedia) and await corroboration against official and professional-chamber sources. Coverage is partial: years without a mark are years without a published, reviewed record — not years without loss.

Where it happened — and where mining happens

Every published record colors its province: the darker the red, the more people died there. Carnations mark the records whose sources state coordinates (facility-approximate — the mine or its vicinity, never an exact point); records without published coordinates are inside their province's shade — rest on a province to see them. Diamonds are documented mining sites from open structured sources, colored by what is mined; sites without published coordinates are counted in the province tooltips and listed in the table below. Documented mining sites

Mining and quarrying sites documented in open structured sources (Wikidata, CC0), registered through the same evidence pipeline as the accident records: every row keeps its source link. Coverage is partial — these are the sites open data documents, a fraction of the operations licensed in Türkiye; absence from this table means nothing. Operator and ownership entries appear only where the source states them, with their assertion status; operating status is shown only when the source asserts it. Context entities — operating directorates, regional aggregates, archaeological quarries — are listed here with their type but kept off the operational map. Fuller registers (Global Energy Monitor, MAPEG licence data) are open assessment tasks.
